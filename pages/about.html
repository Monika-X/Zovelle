The Hands Behind the House

Creative Direction

Two distinct voices, one shared standard. Meet the designers shaping every Zoveelle silhouette.

Elena Rossi

01

Head of Womenswear

Click to read more

A graduate of Central Saint Martins, Elena Rossi has spent two decades perfecting the art of draped silhouette. Her hands-on direction bridges the atelier and the runway, ensuring every Zovelle piece balances sculptural drama with an effortless everyday ease.

Julian Vance

02

Head of Menswear

Click to read more

Trained on Savile Row, Julian Vance brings a disciplined, architectural approach to menswear. He oversees our made-to-measure ateliers and is the voice behind Zovelle's structural tailoring, known for sharp lines, fluid cloth, and a fit that moves with the wearer.

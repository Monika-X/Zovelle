Deconstructing the Modern Silhouette

By Elena Rossi, Head of Womenswear

In an era dominated by noise, clarity becomes the ultimate luxury. As we approached the design phase for the Fall/Winter 2027 collection, the central question was not what to add, but what could be meticulously stripped away.

The modern silhouette, as defined by the House of Zovelle, is rooted in architectural principles. We treat fabric not merely as a covering, but as a structural material capable of defining space around the human form.

"We are not designing garments; we are engineering confidence through geometry and textile."

Take, for instance, the evolution of our signature camel trench coat. Traditionally characterized by excessive epaulettes, belts, and hardware, our reimagining focuses purely on the drape of the heavy wool-cashmere blend and the precision of the raglan shoulder seam.

Size Guide

Find Your Fit

For the most accurate result, measure in front of a mirror with a soft tape. Keep it snug but not tight, and measure over undergarments only.

  • Bust

    Around the fullest part of the chest, keeping the tape level.

  • Waist

    Around the natural waistline, one finger's width of ease.

  • Hips

    Around the fullest part of the hips, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
